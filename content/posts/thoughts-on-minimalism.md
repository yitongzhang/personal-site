Minimalism in design isn't about having less for the sake of having less. It's about being intentional with every element you include.

## Less is More

When we remove the unnecessary, what remains becomes more powerful. Every line, every word, every element serves a purpose.

In web design, this translates to:

- Clean layouts
- Thoughtful typography
- Purposeful use of whitespace
- Focus on content over decoration

## Finding Balance

The challenge isn't in removing everything—it's in knowing what to keep. Minimalism requires careful consideration of what truly adds value.

Sometimes a simple solution is harder to achieve than a complex one. It takes discipline to resist adding "just one more thing."

## In Practice

I've tried to apply these principles to this blog. The focus is on the writing, with minimal distractions. No unnecessary animations, no cluttered sidebars, just content.
