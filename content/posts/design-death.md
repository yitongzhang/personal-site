---
title: "Design is dead, long live Design"
date: 2025-10-02
---

Instantly generating production quality product design will be solved within a year. The path there is pretty straightforwardly more post-training on taste for coding models.

When you can instantly conjure high fidelity designs in code, product design will be unbundled into product management and frontend development. You're either describing the product in words or implementing it in code. We won't need a translation layer in svgs.

.fig and .sketch are end of life artifacts that will be preserved only in the living museums of large enterprises.

As a result, I'm generally pretty bearish all tooling centered around the svg drawing workflow. The gold standard design artifact has always been a working prototype. Where this used to be accessible only to a rarified few hybrid designer / engineers, it is now easy for anyone to do.

## What does this mean?
The end of design as production. Design is really how it works. It's about breathing intent into objects. There's some level of godliness to the work.

How it works is certainly less than ever how it looks.

It's worth rethinking the job of designers, which parts get automated, and who remains to do what. 

Direction setting
Visual asset creation
Alignment seeking.
