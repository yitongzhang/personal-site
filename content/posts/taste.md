When anyone can make anything with AI, most jobs will boil down to taste. But what the heck is taste and how does one cultivate it?

Taste is intuitive, but it isn't subjective. It is an opinion, but not a personal preference. 

So what is taste? Simply put, taste is the ability to discern beauty.

Why is Taste so important
* Beauty is universal – it moves us all, even when don't realize it.
* Taste is leverage. Knowing

Why do some people have bad taste?
* They think taste is mostly subjective and that each person's taste is as valid as the other.
* They confuse the appreciation of beauty with vanity, and there are afraid to develop it lest it renders then vain.
* They have taste in specific domains, but don't bother improving their general taste.

How do you develop taste?
* Taste is not describable knowledge. It's a skill, and like all skills, it is acquired at first through imitation, and refined with practice. It can be useful to think about taste like a sport.
* Learn to identify people with good taste. 
* Slavishly copy what they do. Practice imitation. Don't worry about understanding everything.
* Once you've got imitation down. Take time to understand.
* Notice beauty in all things and learn to seek it. Taste is a transferable trait.
